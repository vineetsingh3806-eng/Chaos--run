# PRIVACY POLICY — CHAOS RUN

**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026  
**Application Name:** CHAOS RUN  
**Package Name:** `com.aistudio.chaosrun.kxmjzq`  
**Platform:** Android (Google Play Store)  

---

## 1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

CHAOS RUN is designed from the ground up as a **privacy-first, offline 2D adventure platformer**. We respect your privacy and believe that games should provide high-quality entertainment without monitoring, tracking, or collecting personal information.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or account identifiers.
- **No Device Identifiers or Telemetry**: We do not collect Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, IP addresses, or device serial numbers.
- **No Location Data**: We do not request or access GPS, coarse location, or Wi-Fi triangulation.
- **No Biometrics, Camera, or Audio Recordings**: We do not access camera, microphone, or biometric sensors.
- **No Financial Data**: The initial release contains zero in-app purchases, zero advertisements, and zero payment processing.

---

## 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

CHAOS RUN operates 100% on-device. The only data processed is non-personal gameplay progress, stored exclusively in your device's private `SharedPreferences` sandbox:
1. **Level Progress**: Unlocked worlds and completed levels.
2. **Game Achievements**: Stars earned (0 to 3 per level) and coins collected.
3. **Speedrun Records**: Best completion times in milliseconds.
4. **User Preferences**: Background music ON/OFF toggle, Sound effects ON/OFF toggle, and volume sliders.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the application or clear app data via Android Settings.

---

## 4. Android System Permissions

CHAOS RUN adheres to the principle of least privilege. The application declares **zero dangerous permissions** in `AndroidManifest.xml`:
- No `INTERNET` permission.
- No `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` permission.
- No storage or media permissions (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` are not used).
- No notification permissions (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` is not requested).

---

## 5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The application does not include any third-party tracking, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or advertising networks (e.g., no Firebase Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no Google AdMob, no Unity Ads).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Because CHAOS RUN collects zero personal data, requires zero accounts, and presents non-violent, cartoon-style retro adventure gameplay, it is safe for players of all ages, including children under 13.
